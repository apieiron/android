Survival Path X
Offline Decision Engine

An interactive, offline decision support system driven by branching path logic. It guides you step-by-step through high-pressure situations—such as wild animal encounters, severe weather, or sudden injuries—helping you evaluate threats and make life-saving decisions under stress.

Elite Guardian
Tactical Command & Offline Comms

An autonomous local command and communication center designed for security networks, emergency teams, and families. It establishes a secure, encrypted WebSocket network over local Wi-Fi, enabling decentralized messaging, offline mapping, QR-based secure device pairing, and real-time critical infrastructure tracking without relying on external internet or global servers.

Secure. Local. Autonomous.

  • Decentralized Comms: Encrypted WebSocket messaging over local Wi-Fi.
  • Offline Map Caching: Download map regions for navigation and asset tracking.
  • Device Pairing: Securely add personnel via QR codes and one-time PINs.
  • Infrastructure Control: Monitor generators, solar power, and critical systems.
  • Emergency Procedures: Manage protocols and broadcast instant panic alerts.
