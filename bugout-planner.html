BugOut Planner

Scenario-Based Survival Planning App

BugOut Planner is a survival and emergency preparedness app designed for realistic planning — not static checklists.

Create custom scenarios and build accurate bug out bags based on real conditions, then track your supplies with full control.

Key Features

  • Scenario-based bug out bag creation
  • Custom parameters: people, days, weather, disaster or war scenarios
  • Pet support (cats & dogs)
  • Manual inventory & home storage tracking
  • Expiration date tracking with notifications
  • Dashboard for expired and critical items
  • PDF export for scenarios
  • Offline-first, privacy friendly
  • Backup & restore support
  • Dark mode & language selection

Who Is This App For?

BugOut Planner is built for preppers, survivalists, families preparing for emergencies, and anyone who wants structured readiness.

Why Scenario-Based Planning?

Every emergency is different. BugOut Planner adapts your gear and supplies to real situations instead of one-size-fits-all lists.
