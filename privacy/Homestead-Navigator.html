Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 17, 2026

Homestead Navigator: Land & Law (also known as "Köye Göç ve Emlak Rehberi" in Turkey) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the application.

1. Information Collection and Use

Homestead Navigator does not collect, store, process, or share any personal or non-personal user data.

  • No personal information is collected.
  • No analytics or tracking is performed.
  • No location data is accessed.
  • No device identifiers are gathered.
  • No registration or account creation is required.

The application operates entirely offline and does not communicate with any external servers.

2. Permissions

Homestead Navigator does not request sensitive permissions. Basic permissions required by the Android operating system are used solely for the proper functioning of the app and do not serve any data collection purpose.

3. Third-Party Services

The app does not use any third-party services for advertising, analytics, or crash reporting. No data is shared with third parties.

4. Children's Privacy

Homestead Navigator does not knowingly collect any data from children under the age of 13. Since no data is collected, it complies with all legal regulations regarding children's privacy.

5. Data Security

Because the app does not collect or transmit any data, there is no risk of data breach or unauthorized access.

6. Changes to This Policy

Any changes to this Privacy Policy will be posted on this page. Users are encouraged to review this page periodically.
